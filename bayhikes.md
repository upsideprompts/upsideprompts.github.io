- **Date:** Saturday, August 1, 2026
- **Time:** 1:30 PM PDT (Meet at 1:30pm, Hike time: 2pm)

#### 🏔️ Hike Reinhardt Redwood Regional
- **Date:** Saturday, August 1, 2026
- **Time:** 9:30 AM PDT
- **Group:** East Bay Hikers (Rating: 4.7 ⭐, 103 attendees)
- **Location:** Reinhardt Redwood Regional Park
- **Details:** Most of the hike will be in shade. Gorgeous park with large redwoods — bring your cameras.
- **Length:** Minimum 6 miles
- **Elevation Gain/Loss:** 700 feet (greatest amount of gain before Skyline staging area)
- **Directions:** Take the 13 to Joaquin Miller Rd. Joaquin Miller Rd becomes Skyline. Continue to Redwood Rd and turn left. Continue down Redwood Rd until you see the sign for Redwood Regional Park on the left side, turn left there and go to the end which is Canyon Meadow Staging and park there. Trail head is there.
- **What to Bring:** Water, trail snack/lunch, sunscreen, and a hat
